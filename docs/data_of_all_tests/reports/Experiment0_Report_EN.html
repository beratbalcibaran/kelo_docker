Findings

1. About twice as accurate as the 6 November test. The leave-one-out static offset dropped from 7.0–11.8 cm to 3.8 cm on average, and the alignment RMS from 6.8–8.1 cm to 4.3 cm. The 2.3 m false lock and the jumps above 20 cm seen on 6 November did not occur in this recording. The improvement most likely comes from the new beacon placement and map.
2. No systematic scale error. The distances between all 66 pairs of the 12 points were compared with OptiTrack: RMS difference 4.4 cm, scale 0.989, worst pair 16.5 cm. This replaces the laser distance meter check in the plan, with better precision.
3. Good enough as a reference? Most likely yes. The rule in the plan: the reference uncertainty should be 3–4 times smaller than the errors to be measured. Marvelmind is at ~4 cm; the map errors expected in Experiment 2 are 5–20 cm. For the lower end (5–10 cm) the ratio is borderline, which should be stated as a limitation in the paper.
4. Jitter depends on the position. At most points the std is 1–3 cm, at point 10 it is 6.1 cm. This may be an effect of the beacon geometry; the point positions are visible in the replay below. The second run will show whether this repeats.
5. The Marvelmind map is mirrored with respect to OptiTrack. The alignment only fits when one axis is flipped (left-handed frame). The analysis handles this, so the results are not affected. However, if mm_calibrate / kabsch_2d on the robot only fits rotation and translation, the alignment will fail with this map. The map axis orientation should be checked in the Dashboard.
6. Device clock: good news for Experiment 1. The difference between the hedgehog timestamp and the PC arrival time drifts by 0.92 % (~9 ms/s, ~7.5 s over 13 minutes). After removing the drift, the remaining jitter is only ~4 ms std. So the device timestamp, corrected for drift, gives the measurement time very precisely, and the offset/drift fit from the plan works clearly on this data.

Open points

  • Only one run. The plan asks for at least two. A second run is needed to show repeatability across runs.
  • Stop durations. The plan asks for 60 s per point; after trimming, only 2 of the 12 stops are longer than 60 s (74 s and 108 s), the others are 18–45 s.
  • No point markers in the recording. The stops were detected automatically (speed < 2 cm/s, at least 10 s). This does not affect the results, but markers would help for the paper table.
  • The hedgehog mount offset was estimated from the data: 15.3 cm from the rigid body pivot. It should be checked with a tape measure; a mismatch goes into the uncertainty budget.
  • The hedgehog height in the Dashboard is wrong: z = 238.5 m is reported. This does not affect the 2D analysis but the setting should be fixed.
  • Latency cannot be measured from this recording. The two estimates from the moves between points disagree (0.14 s and 1.14 s); there is too little motion. Latency is the subject of Experiment 1.
  • Three other recordings from today (deney0_add, deney0_tur1, deney0_tur1_v2) have not been analysed yet.
